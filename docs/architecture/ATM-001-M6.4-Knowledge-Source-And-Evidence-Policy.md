## 2. The corpus is not knowledge

The 846 legacy procedure identities are machine-generated placeholder material
sharing constant metadata. Every one fails the ratified quality rubric on at
least three dimensions: acceptance criteria (846/846), safety content (846/846)
and frequency realism (846/846); 552 additionally fail equipment specificity.
The legacy interval values (`monthly` / 1 / `month` / 60 minutes) are
`GENERATED_DEFAULT` on all 846 with no independent evidence.

**Therefore legacy content is a drafting and reference aid. It establishes no
engineering fact by itself.**

## 3. D01 — meaning of `TRANSFORM_CONTENT` (RATIFIED)

The **231** `TRANSFORM_CONTENT` identities are **accountable-authoring /
revision candidates**.

They are **NOT** approved engineering knowledge. They are **not**
transform-ready. Legacy-generated text may be used as a drafting or reference
aid, and establishes no engineering fact by itself.

The required lifecycle is:

```
legacy drafting / reference material
    → accountable engineering authoring
    → evidence attachment
    → review
    → approval
    → immutable version
    → publication
```

**No automatic transformation of legacy content into authored knowledge is
permitted.** A record may not move from `legacy_generated` to a published
governed version except through accountable authoring with attached evidence.

This resolves M6.1 register entry `M6R1-D01` (recorded there with
`owner_decision_required: true`). The M6.1 record of both the rubric verdict
(`Reject` for all 846) and the separate disposition remains faithful; this
document fixes the *meaning* of the disposition, which the register left open.

## 4. D06 — admissible engineering and safety sources (RATIFIED)

Atiman knowledge may be established from **appropriately scoped and reviewed**:

- regulatory sources;
- OEM / manufacturer technical sources;
- recognized engineering and safety standards and codes;
- authorized customer engineering sources;
- organization-approved procedures;
- accountable qualified subject-matter-expert authorship;
- Atiman-authored engineering content reviewed and approved by qualified humans.

**Rights and licensing govern what source material may be stored, reproduced,
derived from, or redistributed.**

**Legacy-generated content and AI output are NOT establishing evidence.**

The **47 provenance-free legacy safety inputs** remain **non-authoritative
drafting references** and **MUST NOT be promoted as safety evidence**. They may
become usable only when a qualified human attaches an admissible source and the
safety review records that attestation.

This resolves M6.1 register entry `M6R1-D06`.

### 4.1 Unresolved legal / licensing questions (recorded, not answered here)

This policy deliberately encodes **no universal legal conclusion** about
copyright, quotation, paraphrase, standards licensing or redistribution. The
following require separate adjudication before the corresponding material is
stored:

1. Whether paraphrase-with-citation of a licensed standard is permissible in all
   target jurisdictions.
2. Whether OEM manual excerpts may be stored for a customer that owns the asset.
3. Whether customer-supplied documents carry redistribution rights beyond the
   supplying tenant.
4. Retention and erasure obligations for source files held in `uploaded_files`.

## 5. Evidence model (RETAINED, not replaced)

The existing M6.3 source / evidence / confidence / AI-disclosure model is
retained unchanged, with the single addition ratified by ATM-001 M6.4 Step 3B-B
(migration 021, `021_ai_assistance_disclosure.sql`): the AI-assistance
disclosure is now **declared on the working definition while it is a draft** and
frozen from there into the version, so a human reviewer and approver see it
before approving, and the retired publish-time disclosure parameters can no
longer introduce an attribution the approver never approved. Everything listed
below is otherwise unchanged:

- `knowledge_sources` → `knowledge_source_versions` as the authority + edition
  registry (source category, issuing organization, version designation,
  reference number, publication and effective dates, external URI, content
  hash);
- `knowledge_template_evidence` (working) and
  `knowledge_template_version_evidence` (frozen) carrying
  `section_or_clause`, `page_or_paragraph`, `derivation_notes`,
  `confidence_level` and `supporting_role`;
- `ai_assisted` + `ai_assistance_detail`, frozen into versions;
- `trigger_basis_source_version_id` for an asserted trigger's basis.

**No new database evidence hierarchy is introduced by this mission.** Authority
mapping is **policy and documentation, not schema**: the existing
`source_category` vocabulary (`manufacturer_manual`, `engineering_standard`,
`internal_standard`, `regulatory_source`, `legacy_migration`,
`engineering_authored`) plus `supporting_role` and `confidence_level` express the
required distinction without a new enum.

## 6. AI authoring boundary (RATIFIED)

AI **may**: organize source material; compare sources; draft text; translate;
suggest classifications; identify gaps in evidence; assemble candidate
citations; recommend items for human consideration.

AI **MUST NOT autonomously establish**:

- engineering correctness;
- safety controls;
- maintenance triggers or intervals;
- acceptance criteria;
- Equipment-Type applicability;
- source authority or admissibility;
- approval.

**AI-generated material is not its own evidence source.** AI output is always
disclosed (`ai_assisted` with `ai_assistance_detail`) and is never recorded as a
`knowledge_source`. Accountable humans decide.

**Disclosure semantics (recorded by ATM-001 M6.4 Step 3B-B; already-implemented
behaviour, not new policy).** `ai_assisted = TRUE` means AI materially helped
produce or modify the knowledge content; spell-checking, formatting, navigation,
search and deterministic validation do not count. The disclosure detail is a
**structured JSON record** (`ai_assistance_detail`): when `ai_assisted = TRUE` the
record must carry meaningful content — at least one non-blank value — and arrays
and scalars are not valid disclosure records; the authoring primitive refuses
them with `AI_ASSISTANCE_DETAIL_INVALID`, and a record with no meaningful content
with `AI_ASSISTANCE_DETAIL_REQUIRED`. `NULL` is **not** `FALSE`: `NULL` means the
status was never captured under this regime and is never rewritten, while `FALSE`
is an explicit disclosure that AI was not materially used. Authored knowledge
must declare the status before it can be published (admission refuses it with
`AI_DISCLOSURE_MISSING`); legacy-generated knowledge is exempt and keeps `NULL`.
The published disclosure is frozen with the version, and
`content_origin = 'authored'` continues to mean accountable-human-authored,
independently of whether AI assisted.

## 7. Trigger and interval policy (RATIFIED)

Generated legacy interval values are **not** engineering evidence and must never
be promoted to authoritative interval knowledge.

- Any **asserted** trigger requires an admissible basis
  (`trigger_basis_source_version_id`), and the cited basis must itself be an
  admissible source under §4.
- Where no defensible trigger basis exists, use the explicit governed
  **`no_fixed_interval`** state rather than inventing an interval.
- The trigger model cannot express a scheduled date, and no date may be
  introduced.

## 8. Acceptance criteria policy (RATIFIED)

Acceptance criteria require either admissible engineering evidence or
accountable qualified-human derivation with recorded rationale.

**`UNKNOWN` is preferable to fabricated precision.** A step with no defensible
criteria must be recorded as criteria-free rather than given an invented
tolerance, limit or pass/fail value.

## 9. D10 — legacy identity (RATIFIED)

For M6.4 legacy procedure operations:

- **`template_id` is the authoritative legacy identity.**
- **`template_code` MUST NOT be assumed unique.**

Known documented collisions: `COMPACT_INSPECTION`, `COMPACT_SAFETY_CHECK` and
`COMPACT_TESTING` each occur twice, spanning two different concepts — type 47
"Compact PLC" and type 72 "Compact Screw". No uniqueness constraint exists on
`task_templates.template_code`.

Whether `template_code` should *become* unique remains **OPEN** and is not a
prerequisite for M6.4 authoring work.

## 10. D02 — accountability (recorded, not invented)

- **`approver != publisher` is already ratified** and is enforced in schema
  (`chk_task_template_versions_approver_not_publisher`, migration 013). This is
  recorded, not newly decided.
- **`author != approver` is a proposed/desired separation.** Controlled evidence
  does **not** show it as previously ratified: `approveTemplate` records
  `reviewer_user_id` and `approver_user_id` as the same principal, so in the
  implemented M1 model review and approval are the same act by the same person.
  This document does not convert a desire into a ratified rule.
- Governance roles observed: `KNOWLEDGE.REVIEW` / `KNOWLEDGE.APPROVE` /
  `KNOWLEDGE.SAFETY_REVIEW` (admin + supervisor);
  `TASKS.CREATE` / `TASKS.UPDATE` (admin only).

**No universal minimum headcount is asserted.** The only minimum logically
implied by enforced constraints is that **at least two distinct accountable
principals must exist** for a governed publication to be possible, because the
approver and the publisher may not be the same human.

Production currently holding a single accountable user is an **operational
publication-readiness issue, not authority to weaken governance**. No user was
created by this mission.

## 11. G1 / G2 — dated architectural debt

**G1 — rights/licence/admissibility status is not structurally represented.**
No field on `knowledge_sources` or `knowledge_source_versions` records whether
material may be stored, derived from, or redistributed. Today this can only be
carried as free text (`derivation_notes`), which is unenforceable.

**G2 — evidence subjects are template XOR step.**
`knowledge_template_evidence` and `knowledge_template_version_evidence` enforce
exactly-one-subject over `task_template_id` XOR `task_template_step_id`, so
**safety-control claims and Equipment-Type applicability claims cannot carry
their own evidence row**. Evidence can only attach at template or step
granularity.

Neither authorizes migration 021 now. **Revisit triggers:**

- **G1** must be resolved before the first procedure is authored from a licensed
  standard or a customer-supplied document whose rights are not already
  established.
- **G2** must be resolved before the first safety control or applicability claim
  is asserted as *type-specific evidenced* knowledge (as opposed to attesting
  controls under the existing template-level safety review).

separate bounded mission.
