**Architecture decision ≠ implementation evidence.** Each row below separates
the two. A behaviour is never treated as ratification merely because code
behaves that way: where a decision is marked `PROVEN_IMPLEMENTED`, the basis is
that a frozen, independently reviewed artifact was accepted and authorized, not
that source code happens to do something.

Classification vocabulary used here:

- `PROVEN_RATIFIED` — a ratification is evidenced by a controlled artifact.
- `PROVEN_IMPLEMENTED` — realised in a frozen, accepted artifact, evidenced by
  that artifact; the ratification record is the acceptance event.
- `OWNER_RATIFIED_IN_M6.4` — ratified by the OWNER during M6.4 and recorded in
  `ATM-001-M6.4-Knowledge-Source-And-Evidence-Policy.md`.
- `OPEN` — no decision evidenced; remains an unresolved decision.
- `DEFERRED` — evidenced as deliberately postponed by a controlled artifact.

## 3. Architecture established at M6.2 (as realised and accepted)

The following is the architecture the M6.3 candidate implements and which was
independently reviewed (VUDA R2 PASS) and authorized for merge and production
deployment. It is recorded here as the M6.2 architecture of record because
migration 020's header identifies itself as the conversion of "the ratified
M6.2/M6.3 maintenance-knowledge architecture" into schema.

1. **Maintenance Knowledge and Inspection Knowledge are constitutionally
   distinct Knowledge Foundation domains.** They share one governed
   knowledge-definition substrate and are distinguished by an explicit,
   immutable knowledge type frozen into each immutable published version.
   Evidenced by the seeded `knowledge_types`: `MAINTENANCE_PROCEDURE`
   ("Maintenance Procedure") and `INSPECTION_TEMPLATE` ("Inspection Template").
2. **The Maintenance Procedure is the primary enduring Maintenance Knowledge
   object**, carried on the governed `task_templates` → `task_template_versions`
   substrate.
3. **Knowledge type and task family are orthogonal** and neither is inferred from
   the other; `task_kind` must never be overloaded as a knowledge type. Seven
   task families are seeded: `inspect`, `verify_safety`, `test_measure`,
   `lubricate`, `clean`, `adjust`, `replace`.
4. **Maintenance strategy** is governed where applicable, with five values:
   `preventive`, `predictive`, `condition_based`, `compliance`, `corrective`.
5. **Applicability is explicit Equipment Type identity only** — no class,
   category, subunit, maintainable-item or asset inference, and no taxonomy
   remapping.
6. **Trigger semantics are structured** and cannot express a scheduled date.
7. **Scope is explicit and has no default**; `shared` and `customer` are
   assignable and `marketplace` is represented but non-assignable.
8. **Content origin is explicit and nullable with no default**, immutable once
   established, and legacy clearance is a separate write-once attributed event.
9. **Publication admission fails closed**, and a published version is immutable.
