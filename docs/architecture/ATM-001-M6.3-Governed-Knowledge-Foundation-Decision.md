## 2. Architecture

The governing principle is that **publication snapshots an already-governed
definition**. The runtime carries ratified values and never invents them: no
knowledge type is inferred from `task_kind`, no task family from legacy fields,
no scope from the presence or absence of an organization, no origin from a caller
omission, no strategy from unrelated legacy semantics, and no trigger from
`frequency_value`. Absent governed data fails publication rather than fabricating
meaning.

### 2.1 Knowledge domains, type and family

- **Maintenance Knowledge and Inspection Knowledge are constitutionally distinct
  domains** sharing one governed knowledge-definition substrate. They are
  distinguished by an explicit, immutable knowledge type frozen into each
  immutable published version.
- Seeded knowledge types: `MAINTENANCE_PROCEDURE` (Maintenance Procedure) and
  `INSPECTION_TEMPLATE` (Inspection Template). The vocabulary is a reference
  table, not a CHECK constraint, so a further constitutional domain is added as
  governed data rather than a schema migration.
- **Knowledge type and task family are orthogonal and never inferred from one
  another.** `task_kind` must never be overloaded as a knowledge type.
- Seven seeded task families: `inspect`, `verify_safety`, `test_measure`,
  `lubricate`, `clean`, `adjust`, `replace`.

### 2.2 Maintenance strategy

Governed where applicable, with five values: `preventive`, `predictive`,
`condition_based`, `compliance`, `corrective` (CHECK-constrained on both the
working definition and the immutable version).

### 2.3 Equipment-Type applicability

- Applicability is an **enumerated set of explicit Equipment Type identities**:
  a working junction (`task_template_equipment_types`) and a frozen version
  junction (`task_template_version_equipment_types`).
- **No** class, category, subunit, maintainable-item or asset inference exists,
  and **no** taxonomy remapping is applied.
- Exactly one primary anchor per definition and per version (partial unique
  indexes `uq_ttet_one_primary_per_template`, `uq_ttvet_one_primary_per_version`).
- Publication freezes the working applicability set; the frozen set does not
  follow later working changes.

### 2.4 Trigger semantics

The trigger model is a structured **engineering recommendation** that
**cannot express a date**:

- `trigger_mechanism` ∈ `calendar`, `hours_based`, `condition_based`, `event`,
  `no_fixed_interval`;
- optional magnitude and unit, and a structured condition criterion
  (parameter / operator ∈ `gt,gte,lt,lte,eq,neq` / value / unit / context);
- an event description for event triggers;
- an explicit `no_fixed_interval` state;
- a **separate** basis: `trigger_basis_source_version_id` (FK to
  `knowledge_source_versions`), required whenever a trigger is asserted
  (`chk_task_template*_trigger_basis_required`);
- consistency constraints forbid mixing mechanism with inconsistent fields.

### 2.5 Knowledge scope and content origin

- `knowledge_scope` has **no default**; `shared` and `customer` are assignable
  and `marketplace` is represented but **non-assignable**
  (`chk_*_marketplace_not_assignable`).
- Scope/organization coherence is enforced: `shared` requires no organization,
  `customer` requires one.
- `content_origin` is nullable with **no default**, immutable once established
  (`trg_task_templates_origin_immutable`), and only authored content — or legacy
  content with recorded accountable clearance — may be approved and published.
- Legacy clearance is a **separate, write-once, attributed** event that never
  mutates origin.

### 2.6 Evidence and provenance

Reuses the M1/M3 provenance substrate: `knowledge_sources` →
`knowledge_source_versions` (the authority + edition registry) →
`knowledge_template_evidence` (working) and
`knowledge_template_version_evidence` (frozen), carrying
`section_or_clause`, `page_or_paragraph`, `derivation_notes`,
`confidence_level` (`established`/`provisional`/`experimental`/`uncertain`) and
`supporting_role` (`primary`/`supporting`).

### 2.7 Immutability, review, approval, safety review, AI disclosure, supersession

- A published version is immutable; the trigger and applicability guards close
  the frozen boundary.
- Human review and approval are attributed; review state machine
  `draft → under_review → approved | rejected`.
- Safety review is an explicit attributed state:
  `not_assessed`, `reviewed_no_control_required`,
  `reviewed_controls_defined`. A version carrying safety controls must be
  attested as defined.
- **AI disclosure** is carried and frozen: `ai_assisted` +
  `ai_assistance_detail` on versions and step versions, and on definitions as the
  mutable draft declaration that publication freezes.
  *Correction (ATM-001 M6.4 Step 3B-B): M6.3 as first recorded stated these
  columns existed "on definitions, versions and step versions" as part of the
  M6.3 foundation. That was inaccurate. The migration history is exact:
  `ai_assisted` + `ai_assistance_detail` were created by migration 009 on the
  three version tables (`knowledge_pack_versions`, `task_template_versions`,
  `task_template_step_versions`), carried later onto three taxonomy tables by
  migration 017 (`equipment_type_external_classification`) and migration 019
  (`equipment_type_identity_resolution`, `equipment_type_term`), and migration
  020 added no AI column at all.
  `task_templates` carried no AI disclosure column until migration 021
  (`021_ai_assistance_disclosure.sql`), so before 021 the foundation froze a
  disclosure on a version but provided nowhere to declare it. No other M6.3
  architecture is changed by this correction, and the earlier wording is
  withdrawn.*
- Supersession and retirement are governed lifecycle states
  (`lifecycle_state_at_publish` ∈ `published`, `superseded`, `retired`, with
  `superseded_by_version_id`).

### 2.8 Publication admission

Publication runs an admission gate **before the first irreversible write** and
fails closed with every reason found. Governed rules: knowledge type, task
family, maintenance strategy, trigger, trigger basis, knowledge scope,
scope/organization coherence, content origin and applicability must all be
present and ratified; safety controls must be attested; approval must be bound to
the material content; and the approver must not be the publisher.

### 2.9 Knowledge Pack membership rule

**A Pack member must be globally applicable (`shared`) knowledge.** A
customer-owned Pack may exist, but customer ownership does **not** authorize
customer-scoped member knowledge during M6.3. Storage and application service
enforce the same rule. Customer-scoped member composition is deferred.

## 3. R1 remediation (storage guards, approval integrity, manifest identity, containment)

Independent VUDA R1 failed the first candidate and reproduced seven findings.
All seven were reproduced before remediation; the remediation is recorded here
because it changed migration 020 (SHA-256 `955bd6f3…` → `c14a3d53…`) with VUDA
authorization. The failed candidate remains in history.

| Finding | Accepted behaviour |
|---|---|
| **R1-1** cross-tenant Pack access | Pack access is organization-aware at the authorization boundary using the **authenticated** organization; a caller-supplied organization identifier is never trusted; shared Packs remain visible to all; a foreign customer Pack is indistinguishable from an absent one |
| **R1-2** approval integrity | The material approval surface covers the governed semantic columns and a deterministically sorted Equipment-Type applicability set, so a governed-field or applicability change invalidates approval while reordering does not |
| **R1-3** frozen applicability immutability | `trg_ttvet_immutable` refuses UPDATE/DELETE of frozen applicability; `trg_ttvet_insert_before_seal` refuses INSERT after the version is sealed, while pre-seal construction remains permitted |
| **R1-4** member scope divergence | Storage enforces the M6.3 rule (`knowledge_pack_membership_scope_compatibility`); the service guard was not weakened |
| **R1-5** scope/ownership durability | A Pack version's scope must equal its Pack's established ownership at every lifecycle state; `trg_knowledge_packs_ownership_durable` makes Pack ownership durable once a version depends on it, while remaining correctable while none does |
| **R1-6** manifest zero-match corruption | Corpus presence is decided by **identity id** and exactness by the full identity, so a reserved identity present with a non-ratified code fails closed and rolls back; the absent-corpus no-op and tolerance of unrelated rows are preserved |
| **R1-7** disposable environment containment | Destructive disposable subprocesses strip libpq and non-test connection variables at both spawning boundaries, so inherited `PG*` cannot redirect them |

Resulting mechanism inventory in migration 020 (1957 lines): 4 tables,
47 constraints, 12 indexes, 8 functions, 9 triggers.

`trg_task_templates_legacy_clearance_guard`,
`trg_task_template_version_publication_admission`,
`trg_knowledge_pack_version_scope_governed`,
`trg_kpvtv_scope_compatibility_insert`,
`trg_kpvtv_scope_compatibility_update`,
`trg_ttvet_insert_before_seal`, `trg_ttvet_immutable`,

## 5. Explicitly out of scope for M6.3

Scheduling, due dates, work orders, labour, materials, inventory, procurement,
cost or permit functionality; per-control safety provenance; procedure
terminology; a generic knowledge registry; a separate inspection governance
stack; marketplace commerce; any AI feature; decomposition governance;
class/category/subunit/maintainable-item applicability.

## 6. Known architectural debt recorded by M6.3 discovery

Recorded here because it bounds what M6.4 may assume; both are policy-visible
and neither authorizes a migration by itself.

- **G1 — rights/licence/admissibility status is not structurally represented.**
- **G2 — evidence subjects are template XOR step**, so safety-control and
  applicability claims cannot carry their own evidence row.

Revisit triggers and treatment are recorded in
