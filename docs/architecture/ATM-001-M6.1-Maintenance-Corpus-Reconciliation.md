**Status:** reconciliation record — candidate for Chief Architect actual-diff review
**Scope:** documentation and machine-readable reconciliation artifacts only

---

## 1. Purpose

This record reconciles Atiman's current maintenance-knowledge corpus against the ratified maintenance-knowledge quality rubric (ATM-013C §5) and against the Equipment Type lifecycle governed by M5R.4B2. It determines, per Maintenance Procedure candidate, whether the existing content is structurally usable, transformable, duplicate/superseded, generated placeholder, demo material, proprietary-excluded, deferred to another domain, or insufficiently evidenced.

It creates no maintenance engineering content. It invents no acceptance criteria, safety controls, frequencies or procedures.

## 4. Rubric implementation (ATM-013C §5, applied unmodified)

The ratified thresholds were used verbatim. Two dimensions required operationalisation because the rubric states them qualitatively; both are documented so they can be reviewed and contested.

| Dimension | Ratified threshold | Operationalisation |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** Equipment specificity | "≥60% of steps are type-specific" | A step is *type-specific* when its exact normalised instruction text occurs in templates belonging to **exactly one** Equipment Type. D1 passes when the type-specific share is ≥ 0.60. This directly measures the rubric's own wording ("not generic electrical/mechanical boilerplate") without a semantic judgement. |
| **D2** Technical meaningfulness | "All steps are actionable and unambiguous" | Every step must begin with an imperative maintenance action verb drawn from the **closed 22-token first-word lexicon** actually observed in the corpus (`check, verify, inspect, confirm, examine, test, measure, perform, record, calibrate, review, analyze, listen, megger, cycle, compare, ensure, execute, sample, conduct, simulate, calculate` — these 22 tokens account for all 3,099 steps). **Disclosed limitation: this is a syntactic proxy for actionability. Semantic unambiguity was NOT adjudicated.** |
| **D3** Acceptance criteria | "≥1 measurement or check step with criteria" | ≥1 step with any of `expected_value`, `min_value`, `max_value`, `unit`, `data_type` populated. |
| **D4** Safety content | "Safety hazards are addressed explicitly" | ≥1 step with a non-blank `safety_note` or any safety flag true, or ≥1 template-level safety control. |
| **D5** Frequency/duration realism | "Values differ from defaults or are justified" | Not the generated default (`monthly` / value 1 / unit `month` / 60 minutes) and no recorded justification. |

Verdict per the ratified classification table: **Retain** (passes all 5), **Revise** (fails 1–2), **Reject** (fails ≥3).

## 7. Duplicate analysis

Four distinct phenomena are reported separately, as required. **Repeated wording alone was never treated as proof that two procedures are the same.**

| Phenomenon | Measurement |
|---|---|
| **1. Duplicate identity** | 3 `template_code` values duplicated (`COMPACT_INSPECTION`, `COMPACT_SAFETY_CHECK`, `COMPACT_TESTING`) and 3 `template_name` values duplicated (`Pressure Filter - Inspection / Safety_check / Testing`) → 12 rows |
| **2. Exact ordered-step-set duplicates** | 528 distinct ordered step-set signatures for 846 candidates → **318 redundant candidates**; 457 signatures are singletons and **71 are multi-member**, holding **389 candidates**; largest identical group = **22 candidates** (2 such groups) |
| **3. Reused generic step text** | 1,521 distinct normalised step texts; **1,843 of 3,099 step instances (59.5%)** reuse text that also appears in another Equipment Type; 1,256 (40.5%) are unique to one Equipment Type |
| **4. Legitimate common maintenance instruction** | 1,200 of 1,521 distinct texts (78.9%) occur in exactly one Equipment Type; 40 texts occur across 11–50 types and are the true boilerplate population |

**Near-duplicate detection method (documented, deterministic):** Jaccard similarity of normalised step-text sets, restricted to the same `task_kind` and a different `equipment_type_id`, threshold ≥ 0.80. Result: **1,494 exact step-set pairs and exactly 0 pairs in [0.80, 1.0)**. The distribution is strictly bimodal — candidates are either textually identical or clearly different. No opaque similarity judgement was used.

**Taxonomy-driven duplicates:**

- **COMPACT-derived** (6 candidates): types **47 `Compact PLC`** and **72 `Compact Screw`**, both **retired** by M5R.4B2. Their duplicated codes arise from the two case-variant `COMPACT` type codes that M5R.4B2 retired. All 6 → `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` (type-specific content on a retired concept).
- **Pressure Filter-derived** (6 candidates): type **56** (superseded → endpoint **200**) and type **200** (canonical). Both rows are named `Pressure Filter`; codes differ (`PRESSURE_*` vs `PRESS_FILT_*`). All 6 → `TRANSFORM_CONTENT`; the 3 on type 56 resolve to endpoint 200 by immutable identity.
- **Other merges** (14 candidates = 7 superseded types × `safety_check` + `testing`): Storage Silo (205→204), Storage Silo (171→204), LP Feedwater Heater (178→177), Slurry Pump (174→99), Pig Launcher (118→117), Reciprocating Compressor (240→112), TEFC Motor (101→13). For these types the `safety_check`/`testing` step sets are byte-identical to the endpoint while the `inspection` sets are not — so only the identical pairs merged.
